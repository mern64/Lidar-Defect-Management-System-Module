Escalation Resolution Guidelines

As a manager, follow these operational policies when resolving defects backlog warnings:

  • Urgent Hotspots: Reassign high-priority defects immediately to developers with underloaded capacity (utilization below 50%).
  • Stale Reviews: Contact the assigned developer to complete status reviews. Force resolution or reassign if developer remains unavailable.
  • Overdue Backlog: Check due dates of active defects on the scan detail page, adjust deadlines or allocate backup inspector support.
